// 01. CORPORATE PROFILE

ENGINEERED FOR CROSS-BORDER VELOCITY

Founded in 2004, 4PX Express has evolved into one of the world’s foremost international logistics enterprises, pioneering integrated solutions for global manufacturers, brands, and supply chain operators.

With global headquarters established in the technology capital of Shenzhen, China, and high-capacity U.S. Operations based in Los Angeles, California, 4PX Express anchors the world’s most demanding transpacific trade lanes.

Our core mission unites speed, compliance, and international scale. We specialize in cross-border freight forwarding, air charter solutions, ocean container logistics, and intelligent bonded warehousing. By synchronizing proprietary supply chain software with strategic port access, we eliminate bottlenecks across US-Asia trade corridors.

From consumer electronics and aerospace components to high-velocity eCommerce fulfillment, 4PX delivers transparent end-to-end cargo visibility, rigorous customs clearance standards, and localized logistics support on both sides of the Pacific.
